How to Respond to the Open Consultation

Covenant of the Commons — Founding Instrument Series

Consultation Period: 27 September 2026 – 26 December 2026 (90 days)

Authority: Michael C. Seidel, Founder

Scope: Documents XVI (Green Paper — Governance Research), XVII (Green Paper — Constitutional Charter of the Commons), and XIX (Public Prospectus v1.2)

What We Are Asking For

The Covenant of the Commons is in Phase Zero: a single founder, zero capital, and a 24-document constitutional architecture that needs external eyes before it can legitimately claim to serve anyone.

We are asking for your critical engagement with three documents:

Document XVI — Governance Research: Are the five research streams sound? Are the eighteen questions the right ones?

Document XVII — Constitutional Charter: Do the perpetual clauses hold? Is the five-seat governance model viable? What breaks first?

Document XIX — Public Prospectus: Is the three-horizon honesty credible? Are the gates falsifiable?

How to Submit

Option 1: GitHub Issue (Preferred)

Navigate to the repository's Issues tab

Create a new issue with the label consultation-response

Reference the document and section you are responding to

Your response will be public and attributed

Option 2: Email

Send to: michael.c.seidel@protonmail.com

Subject line: "Consultation Response — [Document #] — [Your Name or Anonymous]"

Responses will be logged in /consultation/responses/

Option 3: Anonymous Submission

Use the Google Form: [link to be added by 27 Sep]

No attribution required

We will still log receipt and publish a summary

What Happens to Your Response

Acknowledgment: Within 7 days of receipt, you will receive acknowledgment

Logging: Your response is logged in /consultation/responses/ with date, attribution status, and document referenced

Answer: Within 30 days, a substantive response will be published addressing your points

Change Log: By 28 February 2027, a public change log will show what the consultation changed, what it rejected, and what it deferred

Data Handling

We do not sell, share, or monetize consultation responses

Anonymous responses are truly anonymous — we do not log IP addresses or metadata

Attributed responses are published with your name unless you request otherwise

All responses become part of the public record of the founding process

The Honesty Rule

We are not looking for praise. We are looking for the holes — the assumptions that won't hold, the gates that aren't falsifiable, the governance that invites capture, the economics that don't close.
The two reviews this architecture has already survived (6/10 → 7.5/10) were brutal and necessary. We want more of that.
"Criticism is an instrument." — Document XXIII

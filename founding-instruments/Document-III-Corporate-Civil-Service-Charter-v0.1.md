## PREAMBLE

The Charter declares what we are; the Doctrine declares how we read the world; this Instrument declares how we are built. The Council is designed as a sovereign-agency enterprise: a company in lawful form, a civil service in professional substance, an international governmental order in function — operating through autonomous authorized **Units**, functional **Vectors**, and jurisdictional **Zones**, under the perpetual authority of the Trust and the executive check of the Council of Five.

## TITLE I — SOVEREIGN AGENCY AND LEGAL PERSONALITY

**§1.1 Sovereign Agency Status.** The Council is designed as a private sovereign agency: it exercises governmental *functions* — verification, attribution, arbitration, civil administration support, identity documentation, crisis governance — as a chartered enterprise, deriving authority from (a) the Trust's perpetual purpose, (b) the covenants of its members, and (c) the laws of its host jurisdictions. It claims no territorial sovereignty and no monopoly of force; its sovereignty is *functional and covenantal* — the authority of an institution that keeps its registered commitments. **It claims no authority it does not hold (Doc XIX §3).**

**§1.2 Corporate Form.** Corporate personality is distributed across the Nebulous topology (Doc I §2.3): Delaware PPT (anchor) · Swiss Verein (convening) · Wyoming DAO-LLC (protocol/security layer) · Colorado 501(c)(3) (mission arm) · Burgeon Consortium PBC (commercial holding).

**§1.3 Recognition Strategy.** Sequenced: (a) domestic good standing in host jurisdictions; (b) contracts and memoranda with states, municipalities, and international bodies; (c) observer or consultative status; (d) covenant-grade recognition of verification, arbitral, and identity instruments by members. No step is claimed before it exists (the status discipline of Doc XIX).

**§1.4 Immunities and Accountabilities.** Officers acting within commission enjoy the protections of their host entity and member covenants. Sovereign agency confers no personal impunity: all officers are accountable to the Ledger, the Commons Court, and applicable law.

## TITLE II — UNITS: AUTONOMOUS AUTHORIZED ORGANIZATIONS

**§2.1 Definition.** A **Unit** is an autonomous authorized organization of the Council: separately governed, separately budgeted, holding a written Commission and registered commitments. Autonomy is real — Units set operations, staffing, methods — and bounded — by Commission scope, Trust covenants, and firewall rules.

Failed Units enter the bankruptcy protocol: commitments audited, lessons published, assets and personnel reabsorbed.

## TITLE III — VECTORS: FUNCTIONAL LINES OF ACTION

**§3.1 Definition.** A **Vector** is a cross-Unit functional line. Units are *who acts*; Vectors are *what the order is doing in the world*.

**§3.2 The Seven Vectors (unchanged in substance):** V-1 Verification & Truth (lead U-1) · V-2 Humanitarian Renewal (U-2) · V-3 Security & Resilience (U-3, re-scoped) · V-4 Justice & Arbitration (U-4) · V-5 Statecraft & Transition (U-5) · V-6 Formation & Knowledge (U-6) · V-7 Capital & Enterprise (U-7/Burgeon).

**§3.3 Vector Coordination.** Each Vector has a Director appointed through the Guardian Function; Vector plans are registered commitments; cross-Unit conflicts resolve to the Director, cross-Vector to the Guardian Function, mission questions to the Trust, deadlocks to the Council of Five.

**§3.4 Vector Doctrine.** Each Vector maintains a published doctrine statement — authorities, methods, red lines, falsifiable claims — reviewed annually against the Ledger.

## TITLE IV — ZONES: THE JURISDICTIONAL GEOGRAPHY

**§4.1 Definition.** A **Zone** is a geographic or jurisdictional theater of operations — where Units and Vectors meet actual polities and populations.

**§4.2 Zone Classes (unchanged in substance):** Z-1 Anchor (host-jurisdiction operations) · Z-2 Covenant (member territories, full services) · Z-3 Engagement (assessment and limited service) · Z-4 Intervention (active Article V operations under the Posture Ladder) · Z-5 Frontier (ungoverned, contested, or collapsed spaces; identity, protection, emergent-administration support).

**§4.3 Zone Governance.** Each Zone holds a Zone Charter issued under Council of Five authority: operating authorities, Unit presence, applicable law, relationship to local sovereigns, review schedule. Z-4/Z-5 operate under the Red Dawn Protocols (Doc II, Part IV) and the layer doctrine (Doc X Title II): white/gray/black visibility classes, with black a confidentiality classification and never a legality classification.

**§4.4 Zone Transitions.** Zones reclassify by evidence: Z-3 becomes Z-2 by covenant; Z-5 matures through the emergent-administration pathway toward covenant standing. **The geography heals upward** — every Zone's trajectory points toward covenant standing, measured by the registered metric: population-weighted Zone transitions per five-year period (Doc VII, Part VI.3).

**§4.5 Economic Content.** Zone development attaches to the engine per the mobile-first staging of Doc XXII: the mobile processing unit is the frontier instrument; stationary infrastructure follows proven toll volume.

## TITLE V — THE CIVIL SERVICE CORPS

**§5.1 The Corps.** The Covenant Civil Service Corps — commissioned by the Academy (U-6), serving across Units, Vectors, and Zones. **At formation, the Corps is a corps of one: the founder, delivering professional services under oath-grade standards through the bootstrap agency (Doc XXII §2).** Growth follows earned revenue and Phase gates, not aspiration.

**§5.2 Grades and Tracks.** Three tracks — Stewardship, Operations, Technical. Grades by examination and service record; promotion by merit; leadership by demonstrated commitment-keeping.

**§5.3 The Oath (retained verbatim).** To the Common Cause of Dignity, Liberty, and Happiness; to the registered commitments of office; to the firewall rules; and to leave every institution touched better than it was found.

**§5.4 Discipline and Removal.** Officers removable for cause, subject to Commons Court review. Ledger failure is not automatically personal failure — the bankruptcy protocol distinguishes honest failure from breach of duty.

**§5.5 No Officer Stateless.** Corps members hold protected standing in service — the order's promise demonstrated in its own personnel policy.

**§5.6 Congregations Interface.** Corps members may hold Congregation membership as ordinary members (Doc XXV); no Corps office confers Congregation office; the founder's own rule applies (no office beyond ordinary membership after first rotation).

## TITLE VI — GOVERNANCE OF THE ORDER

**§6.1 Hierarchy of Authority (reconciled).** (1) The Trust — perpetual mission sovereignty; (2) the Network Congress — covenant legislation; (3) the Council of Five — executive decision (three votes); (4) the Guardian Function — doctrine, standards, commissioning recommendation; (5) Unit Commissions and Vector Directorates — operations; (6) Zone Charters — territorial application.

**§6.2 Decision Rules.** Routine: Unit autonomy. Cross-Unit: Vector Directors. Doctrine/standards: Guardian Function. Strategic: Council of Five. Constitutional: two-thirds Congress plus Council concurrence plus Trust non-objection. Mission-critical: Trust veto, unappealable. Emergency: the amended Compression Clause (Doc XIII §2).

**§6.3 Transparency.** Commissions, Zone Charters, methodologies, ledger entries, and failure audits are public per the ledger's five classes (public / internal / restricted / sealed / time-embargoed — Doc XIX §4). Sealed matters carry public markers and opening dates.

**§6.4 The Ledger as Constitution-in-Practice.** What the Council has promised, to whom, by when, at what cost, with what failure threshold — and the public record of what happened.

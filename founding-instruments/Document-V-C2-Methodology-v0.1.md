## PREAMBLE

The institutions that held continuity across millennia did so not by rigidity but by a solved tension: **a consecrated center that embodies continuity, wrapped around distributed cells that do the living work.** Parishes and legions act; the center consecrates, arbitrates, remembers, and endures.

This Document constitutes the Foundation's **Decentralized Command and Control (DC2)** system: decisions flowing top-to-bottom and bottom-to-top through decision-making nodes and actors, arrayed as the legion worked the rhombus — the **Diamond** — beneath a consecrated apex that is *plural by design*: the Council of Five. All grants of command function remain subject to the Anchor, the Compression Clause, exit rights, the layer doctrine, and the firewall rules.

## TITLE I — THE DIAMOND: C2 TOPOLOGY

**§1.1 The Rhombus Principle.** The legion's strength was the *formation that could think*: independent maniples able to hold, wheel, reinforce, or exploit without awaiting orders — because every commander knew the intent, the neighbors, and the standard. The Diamond generalizes this: decision nodes arrayed at four functional points, with bidirectional flow between all.

**§1.2 The Four Points of the Diamond.**

```
                    CONSECRATE
            (Council of Five & the Anchor)
                 ▲             ▲
                 │             │
   SENSE ◄───────┤             ├───────► DECIDE
 (Field nodes,  │             │   (Guardian Function,
  U-1, Zones,   │             │    Congress, Vector
  N-1 actors,   │             │    Directors, Borea)
  Congregations)│             │
                 │             │
                 ▼             ▼
                     EXECUTE
              (Service nodes, Corps,
               Zone operations, engine)
```

- **SENSE (bottom):** every node is a sensor — field actors, Zone nodes, U-1 verification, N-1 citizens, and the Civic Congregations as standing ground-truth organs (Doc XXV). No decision point may be blind to the layer below it;
- **DECIDE (right):** deliberative points — Guardian Function, Congress, Vector Directors, Unit commissions;
- **EXECUTE (bottom-center):** service nodes and the Corps act with maniple-level autonomy inside declared intent;
- **CONSECRATE (top):** the Council of Five and the Anchor (Doc XIII) — continuity, legitimacy grant, succession, and the final word on whether an action *is* the network's own.

**§1.3 Bidirectionality as Law.** Information flows bottom-to-top (sense) and top-to-bottom (intent); authority flows top-to-bottom (commission) and accountability flows bottom-to-top (audit). Any node that blocks upward sense-flow commits a constitutional offense — *blinding the Diamond* — triable at the Commons Court.

**§1.4 Intent-Based Command.** Every commission declares purpose, constraints, and failure thresholds — never micromethod. Executing nodes choose means. Disciplined autonomy under shared intent.

## TITLE II — DECISION NODES AND ACTORS

**§2.1 The Decision Node Taxonomy.**

| Type | Name | Function | Analog |
|---|---|---|---|
| D-α | **Sensor Nodes** | Ground truth, early warning, field reporting | Legion scouts / parish clergy |
| D-β | **Coordinator Nodes** | Lateral synchronization across Units, Vectors, Zones | Centurions / bishops |
| D-γ | **Commander Nodes** | Intent issuance within commission scope | Legates / abbots |
| D-δ | **Arbiter Nodes** | Dispute resolution, legitimacy rulings | Commons Court / canon courts |
| D-ε | **Consecrator Function** | Continuity, succession, mission identity — **exercised by the Council of Five per Doc XIII** | The apostolic center |

**§2.2 Actors.** Every actor holds a defined **C2 role** recorded on the ledger: what they may sense, decide, command, commit. Roles are earned through the Academy (U-6), bounded by commission, audited by standing. No actor holds power outside a role; no role exists without a registered commitment.

**§2.3 The Centurion Principle.** The consequential rank is the middle one. Coordinator nodes (D-β) are selected primarily from proven field actors (D-α experience required). The Diamond breaks at the middle before it breaks anywhere else; **institutions are held by their middle commanders.**

**§2.4 Span and Cell Limits.** No coordinator commands more than seven subordinate nodes without an intermediate layer. Cells below the Diamond operate semi-autonomously under intent. The rhombus re-forms around any three surviving points. Congregations divide rather than exceed their span (Doc XXV §2.1) — the same law at civic scale.

## TITLE III — THE CONSECRATED CENTER (SUPERSEDED AND RESTATED)

**§3.1 Supersession.** The single Constitutional Steward of v0.1 is superseded by the **Council of Five** (Document XIII, governing): the Keeper (life seat — continuity, pardon, warning), the Builder (ten years — consecration of Units and Zones, reserve arbitration), the Sentinel (five years — crisis co-signature), and the two Councillors. What this Title preserves from the original design:

**§3.2 The Conclave.** Succession machinery as designed: four colleges — Guardians, Zone representatives by sortition, Academy fellows, citizen panels by sortition — electing Consuls by two-thirds of at least three colleges (Doc XIII §1.6). The Conclave is seated at founding, not at first vacancy, and meets annually — the succession machinery proven while its first subjects still serve.

**§3.3 The Accountability of the Center.** The consecrated center remains the most constrained element: no operations command, no treasury control, no off-ledger action, no amendment of perpetual clauses. Removal-for-cause jurisdiction sits with the Commons Court; court independence (appointment isolation, firewalled funding, claimant standing, interim suspension power) is **ratification-blocking** — no Keeper may be seated before it is operational (Doc XX §III).

**§3.4 The Audit Regime.** The apex's standing, compensation, and related-party transactions are published quarterly and audited by U-1 with external review — applying to all five seats what was first designed for one.

**§3.5 Mercy.** The pardon and reconciliation power — the order's institutionalized grace — resides with the Keeper (Doc XIII §1.2), audited like every other power.

## TITLE IV — DC2 IN OPERATION: THE DECISION CYCLE

**§4.1 The Cycle.** All network action runs the timestamped loop:

> **SENSE → ORIENT → DECIDE → CONSECRATE (when required) → ACT → AUDIT → (return to SENSE)**

**§4.2 Tempo Rules.** (a) Field nodes act on standing intent without waiting — initiative is the default at the edge; (b) the center's job is tempo, not tasks: accelerate sense-flow, shorten decide-loops, never bottleneck execution; (c) every beat is timestamped on the ledger — decision latency is a first-class institutional KPI.

**§4.3 Escalation Geometry.** Issues escalate one point at a time — laterally before upward; DECIDE before CONSECRATE. The Council of Five is the last resort, not the first call. **An institution that runs to its center for everything teaches its edges to stop thinking.**

**§4.4 Bottom-Up Command.** Any node may originate a proposal traveling the full Diamond upward; proposals carry their originator's ledger standing as credibility weight. The rhombus works both directions because the bottom is where the truth is.

**§4.5 Wargaming the Diamond.** The Academy maintains standing red teams: decapitation drills (lose a Consul — the Council continues; lose the Council — the Diamond holds), capture drills (a Zone defects — the rhombus re-forms), blindness drills (sense-flow severed — offense declared, Court convenes), compression drills (PR-7 activation, co-signature, sunset, retrospective disclosure), and CI extension per the counterintelligence doctrine (Doc IX §5).

## TITLE V — RELATIONSHIP TO THE NETWORK CONSTITUTION

**§5.1 Settled Amendments.** The amendments v0.1 proposed to Doc IV are now executed in Doc IV Final Form: the Guardian Function gains its consecrator and deadlock mechanics via the Council of Five; PR-7 carries the co-signature rule; Congress holds Conclave duty; governance weighting is unchanged — the apex holds powers, not weight.

**§5.2 The Hybrid Resolved (restated for the plural apex).** A consecrated center is a single point of *meaning*; the Council of Five makes it not even a single point of *person*. Five seats, three votes, enumerated powers, no operations, no treasury, overridable veto, mixed selection, Court removal, sortition in the room. **Continuity without capturability — now without singleness either.**

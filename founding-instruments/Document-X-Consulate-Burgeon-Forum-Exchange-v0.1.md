## TITLE II — OPERATIONAL LAYERS DOCTRINE: WHITE, GRAY, BLACK *(governing)*

**§2.1 The Three Layers Defined.** Contingency readiness requires operating capacity at three levels of visibility — all lawful, all ledger-governed, differing in *disclosure timing and access partitioning*:

- **WHITE — Open Operations.** Fully public in real time: ratings, publications, humanitarian programs, commercial operations, Academy, convening. The overwhelming mass of the enterprise lives here;
- **GRAY — Sensitive Operations.** Lawful activity under time-limited confidentiality: negotiations, security layouts, personnel records, litigation strategy, legally protected donor identities, early-stage covenant negotiations. Disclosure deferred to a defined date (default ≤ 12 months); logged at execution with a public "sealed entry" marker — *the world can see that a sealed matter exists and when it opens*;
- **BLACK — Emergency Continuity Operations.** The narrowest layer: lawful continuity measures under the Red Dawn Protocols — contingency trustees, dormant alternate seats, sealed succession documents, emergency treasury recovery keys, protective relocation of endangered personnel. Activation requires **the Council of Five's crisis mechanics (Sentinel declaration + second Consul co-signature + Guardian concurrence — Doc XIII §2.2–2.3)**; automatic PR-7 sunset rules apply; **every black action faces mandatory retrospective full disclosure** — no exceptions, no permanent secrets.

**§2.2 The Hard Boundary.** The black layer is a **confidentiality classification, not a legality classification.** Nothing enters black status by being unlawful; unlawfulness is disqualifying at every layer. Using layer designations to conceal illegal conduct is the highest offense in the order's jurisprudence, triable at the Commons Court with Trust referral to civil authorities. The governing test: **can this be defended in open court and open press at retrospective disclosure? If not, it is prohibited now.**

**§2.3 Layer Discipline.** U-3 administers classification within its re-scoped Commission (Doc IX §1); U-1 audits classification integrity annually; classification inflation is a ledger-recorded offense. The five ledger visibility classes (public / internal / restricted / sealed / time-embargoed — Doc XIX §4) implement this doctrine at record level.

## TITLE III — THE BURGEON CONSORTIUM *(governing)*

**§3.1 Constitution.** The **Burgeon Consortium, PBC** is the ecosystem's **capital management firm**: the Delaware public benefit corporation holding, managing, and allocating the operating assets, capital, and subsidiaries of the commercial layer.

**§3.2 Functions.** (a) holding company for operating subsidiaries (engine segments E-1–E-7 per Doc VIII FINAL, and successors); (b) treasury and capital allocation under Trust covenants and the 50/20/20/10 rule on distributable surplus (Doc XIX §7); (c) investment management: raising and deploying mission-aligned capital under terms preserving the Anchor's veto and the extraction bar; (d) the Dutch-model architecture: the Trust as *stichting* above the operating holding — mission sovereignty separated from commercial risk.

**§3.3 Investor Doctrine.** Investors buy economics, never mission governance; no share class carries Anchor powers; offering documents disclose the Trust veto, surplus ratios, and exit/fork rights. Capital that cannot accept the mission lock is capital the Consortium does not take. **Nothing in the founding series is an offer of securities (Doc XIX §10).**

**§3.4 Registry of Subsidiaries.** All Burgeon subsidiaries are ledger-registered with public ownership records — the conglomerate practices the beneficial-ownership transparency it would have regulators require of others.

**§3.5 Bootstrap Alignment.** Burgeon's first operating reality is the zero-capital staging of Doc XXII: the services agency precedes the holding structure's full constitution; Burgeon is formally constituted when there is commercial substance to hold — and its mission-lock bylaws apply to the agency from the first dollar.

## TITLE IV — THE MERIDIAN FORUM *(governing; renamed)*

**§4.1 Constitution and Renaming.** The standing convening instrument is the **Meridian Forum** — renamed from the working title "Binderburg Forum," which invited confusion with existing institutions and was retired on external review (Doc XIX §8, Note 5). The Forum is the Council's table where the multipolar realignment is *talked through before it is fought through*.

**§4.2 Design.** Annual principal convening plus regional sessions; roughly 100–150 participants — statespersons, ministers, central bankers, industrial and technology leaders, security principals, civil society — with **minimum one-third Global South participation as a charter requirement**. No imposed communiqués; no votes. The Forum builds the trust substrate; the Council's organs decide.

**§4.3 Attribution Policy (corrected).** Discussions are **non-attributable**; participant attendance is published only with each participant's advance written consent; constituency categories are published regardless. The Forum does not claim strict compliance with the Chatham House Rule, which does not permit publishing participant identity [Prospectus v1.2, Note 5].

**§4.4 The Bridge Function.** The instrument of West–East connection and Global South engagement (Doc II §3.4, the Three Wisdoms): one table where rival poles meet a neutral host with verified data (U-1), no extractive agenda (the Anchor), and no home-government script. Hosted initially under the Swiss Verein.

**§4.5 Integrity Rules.** No participant may procure Council contracts arising from Forum access for 12 months; the Forum never lobbies — it convenes. **Sequencing (confirmed): the founding convening follows first-revenue proof — convene from strength (Doc XXII staging).**

## TITLE V — THE EXCHANGE AND THE TIERED ECONOMY *(governing)*

**§5.1 Ambition Stated, Gates Named.** The Council builds toward an exchange-grade market institution for mission-aligned and frontier-economy securities, verified-development instruments, and network assets. **This is the most heavily regulated ambition in the series** — staged, counsel-gated, sequenced last among major initiatives. No stage proceeds without written counsel clearance and, where required, licensure.

**§5.2 The Tiered Economic System.**

- **Tier I — Centralized Layer:** fiat-denominated, regulated, Burgeon-managed — payroll, suppliers, taxes, contracts;
- **Tier II — Mutual Aid Networks:** Coalition mutual-aid funds, Zone cooperative credit — structured as conventional mutual-aid and cooperative instruments; begins at human scale in the Congregations (Doc XXV);
- **Tier III — Gift Economy:** non-reciprocal flows — grants, volunteer-credit recognition, community endowments — recorded as honored social fact without claiming to be currency;
- **Tier IV — Decentralized Layer (tokenomics), strictly staged:**
  - **Stage A — reputation and record:** non-transferable standing tokens; no monetary value;
  - **Stage B — utility instruments:** internal settlement and access credits; counsel-gated; off speculative markets;
  - **Stage C — the Heart Coin (gift-economy token):** a transferable community token honoring gift and mutual-aid flows, designed to circulate recognition rather than speculation; proceeds only with securities/AML clearance and deliberate structuring outside investment-contract classification;
  - **Stage D — decentralized exchange access:** network instruments on existing licensed infrastructure first; a Council-operated venue only as the final, fully licensed stage.

**§5.3 The Tokenomics Constitution.** Four registered tests before any issuance: (1) **no profit-promise**; (2) **redemption honesty** — the token does exactly what is published; (3) **AML/KYC compliance** at every fiat touchpoint; (4) **mission lock** — holder governance cannot override the Anchor. An instrument failing any test does not issue. **Confirmed: no token activity of any kind occurs during Phase Zero (Doc XXII Part IV).**

**§5.4 The Exchange End-State.** A licensed marketplace where covenant-zone development bonds, verified regenerative instruments, PBC shares, and (Stage D) network tokens trade under U-1 verification standards — the economic completion of the People's Proxy: the venue where the frontier becomes investable *because it is verified*. Realistic horizon: 7–15 years. Registered as a long-horizon commitment, not a promise.

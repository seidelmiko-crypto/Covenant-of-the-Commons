Governance of This Repository

Version: 1.0

Date: 1 September 2026

Authority: Founder, Michael C. Seidel (transitional, per Amendment V-3/A)

1. Who Controls Commits

As of this writing, the founder (Michael C. Seidel) holds sole commit authority. This is a transitional arrangement documented in Document XXI (Phase Zero Founder's Plan) and limited by Amendment V-3/A (Document IX §3):

Transitional term: 5 years maximum (not 10)

Veto override: Simple majority of seated Council (not two-thirds)

Quarterly audit: Published ledger review

Permanent ineligibility: After term, the founder may not hold the Keeper seat again

Commit rule: Nothing is ever deleted. Only superseded with a change-log entry linking to the previous version.

2. The Supersession Rule

Every document in /founding-instruments/ follows this versioning convention:

plain

Version [X.Y] — [Status]

Supersedes: [Document #, Version, Date]

Superseded by: [Document #, Version, Date] (if applicable)

When a document is superseded:

The new version is uploaded with a new version number

The old version remains in place with a SUPERSEDED status

A change-log entry is added to /ledger/AMENDMENTS.md

The document index in README.md is updated

Example: Document VI (White Paper v0.1) is marked SUPERSEDED by Documents XVIII and XIX. It remains in the repository for the historical record.

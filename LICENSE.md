FULL ABOUT DESCRIPTION (Sidebar):
------------------------------------------------------------
The Covenant of the Commons is a proposed decentralized institution — company, civil service, civil society, benevolent order, and research institute — constituted under the Constitutional Charter of the Commons, operating as the Global Stewardship Council for Renewal and Resilience (GSC-RR).

This repository contains the complete founding instrument series: 24 documents spanning constitutional, strategic, executive, operational, and material architecture. All instruments are working drafts under registered amendment, disciplined by one rule: the ledger is the proof.
